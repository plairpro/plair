Privacy

Last updated 15 October 2026

PLAIR (plair.pro) is a feed of small web games. We keep as little about you as we can. This page says exactly what that is.

If you just play

Nothing that identifies you. To count plays, minutes played, likes and shares, your browser makes up a random number for itself and keeps it; we only use it so the same browser can’t inflate the counts. There are no ads and no tracking cookies.

Scores and challenges

Some games send their score to the feed. If you are signed in, we keep your best score in each game with your name, so it can be shown on that game’s leaderboard. If you are not, the score is only shown to you and is not stored. A “Challenge a friend” link stores the score and the name you chose to share. The game itself learns nothing about you from us except the score to beat.

If you sign in to comment

You can sign in with Google or GitHub. From them we receive and keep:

  • your name and profile picture — shown next to your comments;
  • your account number at Google or GitHub — so we know it’s you next time;
  • your GitHub username, if you sign in with GitHub — linked from your comments.

We do not ask for or store your email address, your password or any access to your Google or GitHub account. What you write in a comment is public, together with the time you posted it.

If you save games, we keep that list with your account so it shows up on every device you sign in on. Only you see it — unless you press Share list: then anyone with that link sees your name and the games on the list.

Where it lives

Comments and counts are stored in a database on Cloudflare. The site itself is served by GitHub Pages. We don’t sell or share your data with anyone.

Removing it

Open the comments on any game (or your account under the person icon) and press Delete my comments and account: everything we have about you, saved games included, is erased at once. You can also delete single comments, or write to fazullin@me.com and we’ll do it for you.

If you add a game

We keep the link, the title, the line about it, the cover and the genres you send — that is what the card in the feed shows. If you leave a way to reach you, we keep it only to ask about the game. To stop too many submissions from one place, we also keep a scrambled fingerprint of your internet address that cannot be turned back into the address.

Contact form

If you write to us through the Contact form, your message and the contact details you leave reach us in Telegram. We use them only to reply.
